**Methodology note:** This audit focuses on three classes of items:
1. **Numbered "סלי פרויקטים"** in the three "מרחב ציבורי" booklets (clearly enumerated, easy to cross-check).
2. **"כרטסת שטחים חומים" cards** with `המלצות/הליך נדרש לתכנון` text other than "שטח מוצה, לא מוצע להתערבות".
3. **Macro recommendations** from the dossier / insights PDFs.

PDFs that are largely figurative (no numbered list, narrative or street-by-street tables) were sampled rather than line-counted. The xlsx-derived "פרוגרמה לצורכי ציבור" placements per location_id (see `גוננים א-ו - תובנות והמלצות.pdf` pp.17-18) are the authoritative source for that domain and are out of scope for this audit; the geojson appears to match the xlsx (the source of truth).
